      🌌 E8 Mind: Cognitive Architecture Guide

      What is E8 Mind?

      E8 Mind is a cognitive architecture based on the E8 lattice—an exceptional 8-dimensional geometric structure. The AI's thoughts exist as patterns in this high-dimensional space, visualized here as analytic rotor surfaces with curved bridges and thought paths driven directly by backend metrics.

      Understanding the Visualization

      • Pastel Shells: Concentric layers (3D→8D) represent complexity; color encodes semantic grouping
      • Rotation & Movement: Speed reflects activity; tempo is controllable via the slider
      • Curved Bridges: Bezier tubes between shells show structural coupling and flow direction
      • Thought Paths: Curved trajectories across active nodes shaped by mantle field dynamics
      • Subtle Emphasis: Gentle bloom and opacity changes highlight activation without glare

      Key Metrics Explained

      • Coherence: How organized/stable the thinking is (0=chaotic, 1=highly focused)
      • Entropy: Information diversity (0=settled, 1=exploring many options)
      • Intensity: Emotional/motivational strength of current thoughts
      • Tension: Geometric strain between thought dimensions (cognitive load)
      • Pressure: Risk of collapse from information overload

      How the AI Thinks

      The system operates in a continuous dialogue:

      • Teacher asks probing questions to explore conceptual space
      • Explorer searches for answers and patterns
      • Hypothesis forms from the dialogue and gets tested
      • Memory consolidates validated insights into long‑term structure

      Interactive Controls

      • Mouse Drag: Rotate the rotor
      • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in/out
      • Tempo Slider: Adjust global animation speed
      • View Toggle: Switch between Main and Memory views
      • Timeline Scrubber: Replay recent telemetry steps

      M27.FIELDS: Cognitive Field Dynamics (NEW)

      The system now visualizes three fundamental field theories that govern thought evolution:

      • Semantic Gravity (Φ): Attracts related concepts together in thought-space. Visualized as curved field lines showing how ideas "fall" toward semantic centers. Solved using Conjugate Gradient (CG) method for Poisson PDE.
      • Strong Binding: Creates short-range forces that bind closely related concepts into stable clusters. Like quarks forming protons, ideas form coherent thought-units. Shows bound pairs and color exchange events.
      • Weak Flavor: Mediates transitions between different cognitive states (exploration ↔ exploitation). State machine with Poisson dynamics tracks novelty, reward, and multiray corroboration.
      • Emergent Field Detector (EFD): Identifies new field patterns emerging from the interaction of the three fundamental fields. Scores candidates on coherence, conservation, wave behavior, and complexity.
